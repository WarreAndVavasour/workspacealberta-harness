# Trade fit reference

Use the current subscriber profile: trades and licences, service area, crew availability, project size/capacity, bonding limits, insurance, and relevant certifications. Ask only for missing information that affects this decision. Customer-specific values belong in the current authorized work record, never in this skill.

Compare each mandatory buyer requirement with verified subscriber capability. Any confirmed disqualifying mismatch means `fit-low`; otherwise any unresolved mandatory filter means `fit-maybe`. Use `fit-high` only when the applicable requirements and capacity are verified. Do not treat an absent NAICS code as a mismatch if the scope of work establishes the trade.

Bonding and insurance amounts require currency, type, and source. A missing amount means `fit-maybe` and a next action to inspect documents or seek human clarification. A verified bond requirement above the subscriber's verified limit means `fit-low`. “No bonding found” does not mean “no bonding required.” Distinguish bid security from performance and labour/material payment bonds.

Cohere may extract candidate mandatory requirements from text, scanned pages, diagrams, and site photos. Record source/page and a supporting quotation for textual requirements; label image-derived observations separately. A photograph cannot establish a bond limit, certification, legal obligation, or the subscriber's ability to perform the work. Unreadable text and uncertain units/currency remain unknown, not guessed.

Automated bonding/insurance comparison means comparing cited buyer requirements with the subscriber's supplied, dated records. It is not insurer/surety verification. Report coverage type, amount, currency, exclusions and validity dates only where evidenced. If a certificate is stale or missing, ask for current evidence; do not mark verified from a company description.

Use a fresh authorized profile snapshot for each batch, recording when it was obtained. Real-time capacity is available only when a live authoritative scheduling/profile integration supplies it. Without one, label capacity as subscriber-reported with its timestamp, flag stale records, and avoid claiming real-time matching. Do not share one subscriber's profile across unrelated jobs or tenants.

A missed mandatory site visit can make an otherwise suitable live tender `fit-low`; verify any alternate visit or amendment. An optional visit is not disqualifying. Do not infer capacity, bonding, or willingness to partner from company size or trade alone.
